Corners in Crop and Straighten also move without dragging: press a handle, then tap where its corner goes. Until then the handle stays highlighted, and `Esc` lets go of it before closing the tool. The new `cornerSelected` label holds the text that the edit bar shows and screen readers announce meanwhile.
