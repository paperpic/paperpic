Add the Adjust tool for brightness and contrast. The edit bar offers it by default, and its button opens a ruler that repaints the preview while it moves. Cleaning up the document leaves them out. `PaperPic` adds the `setAdjustments()` method and `adjustments` in `getState()`. `PaperPicLabels` adds `adjust`, `brightness`, `contrast` and `adjustmentLevel`.
