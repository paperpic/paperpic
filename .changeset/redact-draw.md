Redact draws its area with a drag on the image, also over the band that it opens with. Once the area is drawn, moved, turned or resized, a drag on the rest of the image pans it again. A second finger cancels the drawing and zooms, and the keyboard and the taps work as before. The default of the label `redactHint` is now "Draw an area over what to hide".
