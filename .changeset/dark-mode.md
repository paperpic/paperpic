Add a dark theme. The color tokens follow the `color-scheme` of the page, as native controls do: with `color-scheme: light dark` they turn dark when the system prefers it, with `color-scheme: dark` they are always dark, and a page without `color-scheme` keeps the light colors. The packages now support Chrome and Edge 124, Firefox 125 and Safari 17.5 or newer, the Baseline Widely Available browsers that have `light-dark()`.
